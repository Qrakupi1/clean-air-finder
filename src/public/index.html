HOW IT WORKS

Transparent data. Clear comparisons.

01

Data source

Measurements are imported from the European Environment Agency (EEA).

02

Comparable stations

City rankings use urban-background stations, keeping traffic and rural stations separate.

03

Data coverage

A station-day needs at least 50% hourly coverage. A city needs at least 75% eligible days in the selected period.

The period average is calculated from eligible daily city averages. Cities without sufficient data are shown separately and are not ranked. Lower PM2.5 concentration indicates less fine-particle pollution; this comparison is not a complete measure of overall air quality.
